ABOUT US

EV research team from Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember.

Anargya builds and races electric vehicles, from the frame to the battery and controller. This kart is where we test it all: every lap at the ITS circuit tells us what to improve next.

INSIDE THE KART

What you see on the 3D model.

  • Electrical box

    The white box behind the seat, carrying the elektro-ITS logo and the big red emergency stop button.

  • Cooling

    A radiator with a fan at the rear right, fed by the bright green coolant lines.

  • Aluminium side box

    Mounted next to the driver on the left side, low and close to the centre of the kart.

  • Cockpit

    Red steering wheel with a small display, behind an orange-and-black front fairing.
